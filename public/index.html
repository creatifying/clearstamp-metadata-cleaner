    What is a metadata cleaner?

    Photos can carry information beyond the image itself. A phone can save the coordinates where a photo was taken, a camera can record its settings, and an AI image generator can embed prompts, seeds, or provenance details.

    Clearstamp creates a new copy with supported tags removed. You can clean a batch before sharing it, then use the metadata report to check what the original and cleaned files contain.

    What the cleaner removes

    Clear the hidden details attached to your images before sharing them.

    EXIF data

    Camera model, exposure settings, timestamps and device identifiers.

    GPS and location

    Coordinates and geotags that can reveal where a photo was taken.

    XMP and IPTC

    Creator details, captions, keywords and editing information.

    Content Credentials

    Embedded C2PA provenance records, where stored in the file metadata.

    AI generation details

    Generator tags and saved settings such as prompts, seeds and samplers.

    PNG text chunks

    Hidden text fields used by editors and tools such as Stable Diffusion.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Clearstamp free?

    Yes. Every cleaner, batch processing, and ZIP downloads are free without an account.

    What does the image cleaner remove?

    It removes embedded EXIF and GPS, XMP and IPTC, text chunks such as Stable Diffusion prompts, and file-based C2PA credentials. Pixel-based watermarks and visible text remain part of the image.

    Will my image quality change?

    Preserve mode keeps the encoded pixels for supported JPEG, PNG, and WebP files. Format conversion, AVIF, and files that need orientation correction use a fresh browser export. Animated images require preserve mode.

    What do the video, PDF, and RAW tools clean?

    Videos keep their original tracks while common MP4 and MOV metadata boxes are cleared. PDFs keep their pages and lose document properties and XML metadata. RAW mode exports a clean JPEG from the camera’s embedded preview when available.

    Can the inspector tell whether an image was made with AI?

    It reports embedded clues such as generator names, prompts and declared digital source types. Tags can be edited or removed, so an image with no signals may still be AI-generated. The inspector does not analyze pixels or verify Content Credentials signatures.

    Does Instagram remove an existing AI Info label?

    The cleaner works on a new copy of your file. Instagram manages existing posts, disclosure requirements, and labeling decisions. Read Meta’s labeling information.
